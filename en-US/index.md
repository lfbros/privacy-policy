# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the LFBROS mobile application (the "App"). The App is provided by HEILONGJIANG LF BROS TECHNOLOGY CO.,LTD. (the "Service Provider", "we", "us" or "our").

## Information Collection and Use

The App can be used without registering an account. It has no user center and does not operate a server used to collect, store or aggregate users' personal information. When you use the App, the App processes the following information only locally on your device:

- The version of your device's mobile operating system and the system language, used for interface adaptation and permission requests.

- The name, model, connection status and operating parameters of the parking heater device, used for device discovery, connection, control and status display.

This information is stored only locally on your device and is not uploaded to the Service Provider or any third-party server. The App does not collect your Internet Protocol (IP) address, and does not record the pages you visit within the App, the dates and times of your visits, the time you spend on them, or your total usage time.

### Bluetooth Device Information and Location Information

The App connects to parking heater devices via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to control the device power, set the temperature, view the operating status and read device operating parameters. To provide these functions, the App accesses your device's Bluetooth capability when you actively scan for or connect to a device, and processes locally the name, model, Bluetooth address and advertising data of nearby parking heater devices. This information is used only for device discovery, connection, control and status display. The App does not collect information unrelated to parking heater devices, does not use it for advertising or marketing, and does not share it with third parties.

The App **does not collect, store or upload your GPS location information**, does not provide any location-based features, and does not continuously obtain your location in the background. Please note that on Android 11 and lower, the system requires an app to obtain location permission before it can scan for Bluetooth devices; the App may therefore request location permission when scanning for devices. **This permission is solely a precondition for the system's Bluetooth scanning.** The App does not read, record, cache or transmit your latitude and longitude coordinates, and does not use them for any other purpose. On Android 12 and higher, the App uses the system-provided Bluetooth scan permission that is not used for location inference (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, neverForLocation), and scan results are not used to infer your actual location. You can disable these permissions at any time in the system settings; once disabled, the App will not be able to scan for and connect to parking heater devices.

### Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App itself does not use cookies, pixel tags or similar tracking technologies for user profiling, advertising or behavioral analysis, and does not integrate advertising or statistical analytics third-party SDKs.

The in-app web pages are used only to display help content built into the App locally. They do not load third-party web pages and do not use cookies or similar technologies to collect your information.

The App does not require you to provide your name, phone number, email address or other personally identifiable information. Only when you voluntarily contact us by email will we receive the email address and message content you provide, and we will use them only to reply to your question.
